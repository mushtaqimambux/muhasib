Voice input setup
To add items by voice, this app needs a free Gemini API key from Google.

1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey in your browser
2. Sign in with your Google account and tap "Create API key"
3. Paste it below — you can add more than one, one per line. If a key runs out of quota, the app automatically tries the next one.

Your keys are stored only on this phone — never sent anywhere except directly to Google when you use voice input.
